= Setting Up Accounts for The Data Mine

Welcome to The Data Mine! You must follow the steps below to create accounts on the Anvil supercomputer.

Some NOTES before you start:

[IMPORTANT]
====
If you already completed these steps during previous years (for instance, if you have been in The Data Mine in the past, for a seminar or corporate partners course) you do not need to repeat them again!
====

[NOTE]
====
Everyone who will work on seminar and/or corporate partner projects, including all students, TAs, and mentors that choose to, must complete **all of the steps** outlined below. These steps are necessary to create accounts on the Anvil supercomputer and grant access to shared directories for corporate partner projects where applicable.
====



Please follow these three steps carefully. If you have trouble at any point, you can jump to the <<get-help, help section>> at the bottom of this page. We suggest you use the https://www.firefox.com[Firefox] browser on a laptop or desktop for all steps.

[[step-1-create-access]]
== Step 1: Create an ACCESS Account

First, you need to create an ACCESS account. ACCESS is a program funded by the National Science Foundation, to provide a platform for researchers around the country to access supercomputers. It is used by The Data Mine to provide access to the Anvil supercomputer.

*   If you already have an ACCESS account, you do *not* need to create a new one. You can skip ahead to <<step-2-notify-Dr-Ward>>.
*   If you think you already have an ACCESS account but forgot your ACCESS username, you can find it here: https://identity.access-ci.org/username-reminder

To create a new ACCESS account, browse to https://identity.access-ci.org/new-user-direct

. Enter your *name*, and your Purdue *email* address, and click Submit.
. You'll be sent a confirmation code via email, which you'll enter.
. Next you will be asked to enter your *Home Organization*.  If you are Purdue student, select Purdue University. If you are a student at another University or a mentor with a corporate partner, select your respective University or company/institution. If you cannot find your organization in the list, you can request that it be added on the same page.  The email address you provided above must be associated with the institution you select or later steps to create an Anvil account will fail.
. You will be asked to review and accept the *Acceptable Use Policy*.
. It will look like your account creation is complete, but you *must* complete the *ACCESS User Profile* step or we will be unable to create your accounts on the Anvil supercomputer.  You can either click the *ACCESS User Profile* on the page you see after accepting the Acceptable Use Policy, or you can go to https://allocations.access-ci.org/profile to complete your ACCESS profile.  In particular, it is necessary to declare your country where you are studying, i.e., in the United States.
. You will be asked to re-authenticate your session by logging in again, so we must do a few additional steps before actually completing the profile.  You will either see a screen like this, where you will just click *AUTHENTICATE WITH CILOGON*:
+
image::reauthenticate.png[width=792, height=500, role="thumb", loading=lazy, title="Authenticate with CILOGON", alt="A screenshot of ACCESS re-authentication using CILOGON."]
+
Or you will see a screen like this, prompting you to select an identity provider.  You must leave it as the default of *ACCESS CI (XSEDE)*!
+
image::identityprovider.png[width=792, height=500, role="thumb", loading=lazy, title="Select Identity Provider", alt="A screenshot of ACCESS Login screen prompting for Idenity Provider."]
+
. Next you will be asked to enroll in the multi-factor authentication (MFA) system Duo Security which will ask you to install the Duo Mobile app on your smartphone or tablet.  Complete the Duo enrollment steps as directed.
. Now you can complete the ACCESS User Profile.  You must fill in *all* of the required fields marked with a red asterisk.  You must click the *SAVE PROFILE* button for these changes to take effect!

Be sure to record the ACCESS username and password you create. The ACCESS ID should have been sent to you via email from `registry@cilogon.org` with the subject "Your new ACCESS ID".

[[step-2-notify-Dr-Ward]]
== Step 2: Submit Your ACCESS Account name to Dr Ward

Your ACCESS username is usually different than your Purdue username, so you need to notify Dr Ward about which ACCESS username that you were assigned.  Please complete the 3 questions here on Qualtrics, about your name, email, and the ACCESS username that you were assigned:

https://purdue.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_23G64aAAKNshTrE

This Qualtrics survey takes less than 1 minute to complete, because it only has 3 three questions.

[[step-3-login-anvil]]
== Step 3: Log In to Anvil and Start a Server

You will do your work on the Anvil supercomputer through a browser on a desktop or laptop computer. A tablet like an iPad will not work as well. Your computer does not need to be new or powerful. It just needs a modern web browser, preferably Firefox (rather than Chrome, Edge, Safari, etc.).
After you have submitted the Qualtrics survey and added your ACCESS username, you need to (please) patiently wait for Dr Ward to register your account.

After he notifies you that your account is ready, you can try to log in to Anvil for the first time, by browsing to:

https://notebook.anvilcloud.rcac.purdue.edu/

When you log in, you will see "Server Options". Please only choose **Datamine Notebook**.  The other options will not have the right versions of Python, R, and associated packages installed.

image::AnvilDataMineScreen.png[width=792, height=500, role="thumb", loading=lazy, title="Anvil Server Option Screenshot", alt="A screenshot of the Anvil server options page, with the 'Datamine Notebook' option selected."]

Once you successfully login, you will be in a Jupyter Notebook environment where you can create and run notebooks for your Data Mine work.  Unless you are ready to begin creating Jupyter Notebooks, you can simply log out by clicking File->Hub Control Panel->Stop My Server, and then close the browser tab.
