=== `tapply` overview

The https://stat.ethz.ch/R-manual/R-devel/library/base/html/tapply.html[documentation definition] for `tapply` may not seem clear the first time that you read it, because the arguments are `(X, INDEX, FUN)`, where `X` is an object where the `split` function applies, `INDEX` is a factor by which `X` is grouped, and `FUN` is function as before.

To simplify this definition, we can say `tapply` applies `FUN` to `X` when `X` is grouped by `INDEX`. Consider the following: we have a `grades` data.frame that contains information for grade, year, and sex for several students. We can use `tapply` to get the average grade by year in a simple way.

An small example with only 10 rows of data should help:

[source,R]
----
grades
----

----
   grade      year  sex
1    100    junior    M
2     99 sophomore    F
3     75 sophomore    M
4     74 sophomore    M
5     44    senior    F
6     69    junior    M
7     88    junior    F
8     99    senior <NA>
9     90 firstyear    M
10    92    junior    F
----

The solution is given below.

[source,R]
----
tapply(grades$grade, grades$year, mean)
----

----
firstyear    junior    senior sophomore 
 90.00000  87.25000  71.50000  82.66667
----
