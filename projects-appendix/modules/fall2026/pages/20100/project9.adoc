== Introduction to Databases (Part 1)

== Project Objectives

**Motivation:** Starting with this project, we will learn about databases, which allow you to write queries about data.

**Context:** We will focus on SQLite.  Once you understand how to make queries in SQLite, you will have a strong foundation that can be used to learn other database resources, such as MySQL.

**Scope:** SQLite queries that do not need `JOIN`

.Learning Objectives
****
- We will learn how to make SQLite queries on one table at a time (without using the `JOIN`)
****

=== Question 1

a.  How many people from the IMDB database were born in the same year as you?

b.  How many were born in the year that Dr Ward was born (1976)?

c.  Find your favorite actor or actress in the IMDB database.  Check their `nconst` on www.imdb.com (in the URL for their page) and compare it with their entry in the `name` table.  What year were they born?

=== Question 2

a. During the years 1980 to 1990, how many people (from the `name` table) were born in each year?  For each year, show the number of people who were born in each year.  It probably makes the most sense to sort your results by year.

b. During the years 2000 to 2020, how many people (from the `name` table) died in each year?  Again, show the number of people who died in each year, and sort your results by year.

=== Question 3

In which years was the tvSeries House of Cards broadcast?  Hint:  You likely need to limit the results so that the `titleType` is `tvSeries`.

=== Question 4

a. Lookup the `nconst` for Whoopi Goldberg on IMDB from the URL of her www.imdb.com webpage.  Using her `nconst`, how many entries does she have in the `principals` table?

b. She appears altogether with 6 different types of `category`, namely:
`actress`, `archive_footage`, `director`, `producer`, `self`, `writer`.  How many times does she appear in each type of these categories?

=== Question 5

a. Consider the tvSeries The West Wing.  How many episodes of this tvSeries aired altogether?

b. Now, consider your favorite tvSeries: how many episodes does your favorite show have?
